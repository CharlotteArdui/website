charlotte ardui is an architect, researcher
and educator working at the intersections of
architecture, heritage and urbanism. She holds
a PhD in Architecture (KULeuven, 2026) on the
adaptive reuse of post-war parish churches
in Flanders, works as a freelance architect
with Brussels-based office CRIT., and teaches
architecture and cultural history at Hasselt
University. Across these different roles, her
work starts from a careful reading of places
and an interest in architecture as an hopeful
social and cultural act.
