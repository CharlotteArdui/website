About

Charlotte Ardui is an architect, researcher, author and educator working between architectural practice and academic research.

Her work focuses on adaptive reuse, twentieth-century heritage, collective spaces and research by design.

Through architectural projects, writing, teaching and visual research, she investigates how existing places can be understood, transformed and reused.
